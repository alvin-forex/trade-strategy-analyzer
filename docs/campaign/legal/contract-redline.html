1. Services

Contractor shall provide graphic design, front-end development, and related creative services ("Services") as requested by Client from time to time. Client may modify the scope of the Services at any timesubstantially in accordance with the statement of work attached as Schedule A. Any change to the scope shall be made only by a written change order signed by both parties.

Open-ended scope ("as requested from time to time") invites scope creep. Bind scope to a SOW and require signed change orders.

2. Term and Renewal

This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues for one (1) year. Thereafter it renews automatically for successive one (1) year periods unless either party gives the other at least ninety (90)thirty (30) days' prior written notice of non-renewal. Neither party shall be obligated to renew, and non-renewal shall not be treated as a breach.

A 90-day non-renewal window is a trap for a freelancer who misses the deadline. Thirty days is workable; add an express right not to renew.

3. Compensation and Payment

Client shall pay Contractor a monthly fee of USD 3,500 for the Services. Invoices submitted by Contractor are payable net ninety (90)thirty (30) days. Client may withhold any payment, in whole or in part, if Client is not satisfied with the Services for any reasonClient may withhold only amounts that it disputes in good faith, by written notice describing the dispute; the undisputed portion remains payable when due. Overdue amounts accrue interest at 1.5% per month.

Net-90 plus discretionary withholding lets the client defer pay indefinitely. Market practice is net-30, good-faith dispute only, and late interest.

4. Expenses

No change — acceptable as drafted.

5. Intellectual Property

Upon creation, and subject to payment of the corresponding invoices, all work product and deliverables , and all of Contractor's pre-existing tools, templates, scripts, and libraries used in performing the Services, shall be owned exclusively by Client. Contractor hereby assigns to Client all worldwide rights, title, and interest in and to the foregoing, in perpetuity, without additional compensation. Contractor retains all right, title, and interest in and to its pre-existing tools, templates, scripts, and libraries ("Pre-Existing Materials"). Contractor grants Client a non-exclusive, perpetual, royalty-free license to use Pre-Existing Materials solely as embedded in the deliverables.

The original transfers the contractor's own toolchain. That is a career-level giveaway. Keep ownership of Pre-Existing Materials and license them to the client instead.

6. Confidentiality

Contractor shall maintain the confidentiality of all Client information in perpetuity, including any information that is or becomes publicly availablefor three (3) years following termination of this Agreement. Confidential information does not include information that is or becomes public other than by Contractor's breach, that Contractor can show was already known, or that is independently developed.

Perpetual confidentiality covering public information is overbroad and hard to enforce. Use a defined term with standard exclusions.

7. Non-Competition

For five (5) years following termination of this Agreement, Contractor shall not provide services similar to the Services to any business anywhere in the world.For twelve (12) months following termination, Contractor shall not solicit Client's customers of record with whom Contractor had material contact, for the purpose of providing services competitive with the Services. This Section does not restrict Contractor from providing services to any other client.

A 5-year worldwide non-compete on an individual contractor is overbroad and unenforceable in several jurisdictions (e.g., California Business & Professions Code §16600). Replace with a narrow non-solicitation, or delete.

8. Warranties

Contractor warrants that the Services will be perfect and error-free for the entire term of this Agreement, and that Client shall have unlimited remedies for any breach of this Section.Contractor warrants that the Services will be performed in a professional and workmanlike manner. If Client notifies Contractor of a material defect within ninety (90) days of delivery, Contractor shall re-perform the affected Services at no additional charge. This warranty is Client's exclusive remedy for defective Services.

"Perfect and error-free" with unlimited remedies is an unlimited warranty. Replace with a professional-standards warranty and a 90-day re-performance remedy.

9. Indemnification

Contractor shall indemnify, defend, and hold harmless Client from any and all claims, damages, and expenses arising out of or relating to the Services, including claims caused by Client's own negligence, but only to the extent caused by Contractor's negligence, willful misconduct, or infringement of a third party's intellectual property rights. Contractor's indemnity does not apply to the extent a claim is caused by Client's negligence or willful misconduct. Each party indemnifies the other for third-party claims arising from its own negligence or willful misconduct.

Indemnifying the client for the client's own negligence is one-sided risk transfer. Make indemnities mutual and fault-based.

10. Termination

Client may terminate this Agreement immediately at any time, with or without cause, and without notice. Contractor may terminate this Agreement only upon ninety (90) days' prior written notice. Upon any termination, Client shall have no obligation to pay for work performed but not yet invoiced.Either party may terminate for material breach upon fifteen (15) days' written notice if the breach remains uncured. Either party may terminate for convenience upon thirty (30) days' prior written notice. Upon any termination, Client shall pay all amounts earned for work performed through the effective date of termination, within thirty (30) days.

Immediate, free termination for the client plus forfeiture of unpaid work is the harshest clause in the draft. Make termination mutual and pay for work performed.

11. Assignment

Client may assign this Agreement freely. Contractor may not assign this Agreement without Client's prior written consent.Neither party may assign this Agreement without the other party's prior written consent, except that either party may assign to a successor in connection with a merger or sale of substantially all of its assets, with notice.

One-way assignment rights are unnecessary. Make assignment mutual, with a standard successor exception.

12. Dispute Resolution

Any dispute arising out of or relating to this Agreement shall be resolved by binding arbitration in Honolulu, HawaiiNew York, New York, administered by the American Arbitration Association under its Commercial Arbitration Rules. In any dispute, Contractor shall pay Client's reasonable attorneys' fees.Each party shall bear its own attorneys' fees, and the arbitrator may allocate fees only as permitted by applicable law.

A remote arbitration seat plus one-way fee-shifting is a travel-and-cost trap. Use a neutral major city and neutral fee allocation.

13. Governing Law

This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of CaliforniaNew York, without regard to conflicts of law principles.

The client is a New York LLC, the arbitration seat was Honolulu, and the draft picked California law — three jurisdictions pointing in different directions. Align governing law with the client's home state (or Delaware) and keep one seat.

14. Entire Agreement

This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties and may be amended only in a writing signed by Clientboth parties.

Amendments signed by one side only are not amendments. Require both signatures.

15. Limitation of Liability (new section)

To the maximum extent permitted by law: (a) neither party shall be liable for indirect, incidental, special, consequential, or punitive damages; and (b) each party's total aggregate liability arising out of this Agreement shall not exceed the fees paid or payable to Contractor under this Agreement in the twelve (12) months preceding the claim. This limitation does not apply to a party's indemnification obligations, breach of confidentiality, or willful misconduct.

The original draft had no liability cap at all — the single largest risk in the document. Add a mutual cap with standard carve-outs.
