Independent Contractor Services Agreement

Sample agreement for demonstration · Not a real party

This Independent Contractor Services Agreement (this "Agreement") is entered into as of November 1, 2026 (the "Effective Date"), by and between Brightwave Media LLC, a New York limited liability company ("Client"), and Jordan Lee, an individual ("Contractor").

1. Services

Contractor shall provide graphic design, front-end development, and related creative services ("Services") as requested by Client from time to time. Client may modify the scope of the Services at any time.

2. Term and Renewal

This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues for one (1) year. Thereafter it renews automatically for successive one (1) year periods unless either party gives the other at least ninety (90) days' prior written notice of non-renewal.

3. Compensation and Payment

Client shall pay Contractor a monthly fee of USD 3,500 for the Services. Invoices submitted by Contractor are payable net ninety (90) days. Client may withhold any payment, in whole or in part, if Client is not satisfied with the Services for any reason.

4. Expenses

Client shall reimburse reasonable, pre-approved out-of-pocket expenses incurred in performing the Services.

5. Intellectual Property

Upon creation, all work product and deliverables, and all of Contractor's pre-existing tools, templates, scripts, and libraries used in performing the Services, shall be owned exclusively by Client. Contractor hereby assigns to Client all worldwide rights, title, and interest in and to the foregoing, in perpetuity, without additional compensation.

6. Confidentiality

Contractor shall maintain the confidentiality of all Client information in perpetuity, including any information that is or becomes publicly available.

7. Non-Competition

For five (5) years following termination of this Agreement, Contractor shall not provide services similar to the Services to any business anywhere in the world.

8. Warranties

Contractor warrants that the Services will be perfect and error-free for the entire term of this Agreement, and that Client shall have unlimited remedies for any breach of this Section.

9. Indemnification

Contractor shall indemnify, defend, and hold harmless Client from any and all claims, damages, and expenses arising out of or relating to the Services, including claims caused by Client's own negligence.

10. Termination

Client may terminate this Agreement immediately at any time, with or without cause, and without notice. Contractor may terminate this Agreement only upon ninety (90) days' prior written notice. Upon any termination, Client shall have no obligation to pay for work performed but not yet invoiced.

11. Assignment

Client may assign this Agreement freely. Contractor may not assign this Agreement without Client's prior written consent.

12. Dispute Resolution

Any dispute arising out of or relating to this Agreement shall be resolved by binding arbitration in Honolulu, Hawaii. In any dispute, Contractor shall pay Client's reasonable attorneys' fees.

13. Governing Law

This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of California, without regard to conflicts of law principles.

14. Entire Agreement

This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties and may be amended only in a writing signed by Client.
