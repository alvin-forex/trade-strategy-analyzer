### 寫入格式（REST，範例）

```
POST https://qbodlutvtkerogwrmcqv.supabase.co/rest/v1/signals
Headers:
  apikey: <你的 secret key>
  Authorization: Bearer <你的 secret key>
  Content-Type: application/json
  Prefer: return=minimal
Body（陣列，一次最多 500 行）:
[
  {"as_of":"2026-11-14T15:00:00Z","symbol":"EURUSD","action":"BUY","confidence":0.72,

```
